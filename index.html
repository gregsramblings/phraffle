Instant raffles where everyone's already holding a ticket — it's the last four digits of their phone.

Tap a reel to stop it

How to run a giveaway

  1. Get the whole room to stand up.
  2. The reels show the last four digits of a phone number. Everyone stays standing only while the digits match their own.
  3. Tap the right-hand reel to stop it — about 90% of the room should sit down.
  4. Tap the next reel to its left. If nobody's left standing, spin it again and retry.
  5. Last person standing wins.

In a room of 100, it usually takes 2–4 taps to land on one winner.
